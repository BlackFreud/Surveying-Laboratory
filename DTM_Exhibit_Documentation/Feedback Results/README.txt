FEEDBACK RESULTS
================

Feedback_Form_Template.docx is the blank form to hand out (or print) to
visitors and students after they try the exhibit.

As responses come in, save or log them here. For a physical exhibit, either:
  - Collect filled paper copies and store scans here, or
  - Transcribe responses into a spreadsheet (e.g. Feedback_Log.xlsx) with
    one row per respondent.

This folder is intentionally empty of actual responses until real visitors
have provided feedback — no sample or placeholder data has been included.
